## Luxury Yacht and Boat Rentals

![Luxury yacht cruising on the Bosphorus with Istanbul skyline](https://images.pexels.com/photos/40173156/pexels-photo-40173156.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&h=650&w=940)

The Bosphorus is Istanbul's most spectacular feature, and there is no better way to experience it than from the deck of a private yacht. Our luxury yacht rental service offers a fleet of motor yachts, sailing yachts, and traditional Turkish gulets, ranging from 15-meter day cruisers to 40-meter superyachts. All yachts are crewed with experienced captains, deckhands, and onboard chefs who prepare gourmet meals to your specifications.

A private Bosphorus yacht charter typically lasts 2 to 6 hours, with routes customized to your preferences. The standard route departs from Besiktas or Eminonu and cruises north along the European shore, passing Dolmabahce Palace, the Ciragan Palace, Ortakoy Mosque, and the Bosphorus Bridge before reaching the second bridge at Sariyer. Along the way, your captain narrates the history of the palaces, waterfront mansions, and fortresses you pass. For a longer experience, we can cruise to the Black Sea entrance of the Bosphorus and anchor at a secluded bay for swimming and lunch.

Our most popular yacht charter is the Bosphorus Sunset Cruise, a 3-hour experience timed to catch the golden hour and sunset over the city. The yacht departs at 5:30 PM (6:30 PM in summer), serves champagne and canapes on deck as the sun sets, and returns to port as the city lights illuminate both shores. This is one of the most romantic and memorable experiences available in Istanbul, and it is a favorite for marriage proposals, anniversary celebrations, and special birthdays. For an extended experience, we can arrange a full-day yacht charter with a swim stop, a waterfront lunch at a seafood restaurant accessible only by boat, and a sunset return.

Yacht charter prices depend on the vessel size, duration, and season. A 15-meter motor yacht starts at approximately 5,000 TL per hour, while a 30-meter luxury yacht starts at 15,000 TL per hour. All prices include crew, fuel, and basic catering. Premium catering, champagne, and water toys (jet skis, paddleboards) are available at additional cost. We recommend booking at least 2 weeks in advance during the peak season (June to September), as the best yachts are chartered early.

## Private Museum and Gallery Tours

![Visitors admiring artwork in a museum gallery](https://images.pexels.com/photos/15925080/pexels-photo-15925080.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&h=650&w=940)

Istanbul's museums and galleries house some of the world's most extraordinary collections, but visiting them during public hours often means dealing with crowds, noise, and limited time at each exhibit. Our private museum and gallery tour service offers exclusive access to Istanbul's cultural treasures, with expert guides and, in some cases, access outside of public opening hours.

The most popular private tour is the Hagia Sophia and Topkapi Palace after-hours experience. Arranged through our contacts at the Ministry of Culture and Tourism, this tour allows you to explore Hagia Sophia and Topkapi Palace after they have closed to the public, with only your guide and a security escort present. Standing alone beneath the great dome of Hagia Sophia, with no crowds and no noise, is one of the most profound cultural experiences available anywhere in the world. The tour includes the upper gallery mosaics, the Harem, and the Imperial Treasury, with your guide providing detailed historical commentary at each stop.

For art enthusiasts, we arrange private tours of the Istanbul Modern art museum, the Pera Museum, and the Sakip Sabanci Museum, with curators or art historians as guides. We can also arrange visits to private galleries in the Beyoglu and Karakoy districts, where you can meet gallery owners and artists, view works before they go on public display, and even arrange purchases. For collectors, we provide art advisory services, connecting you with reputable dealers and auction houses in Istanbul.

For a more specialized experience, we can arrange a private tour of the Chora Church (Kariye Mosque), which houses some of the finest Byzantine mosaics and frescoes in the world. Your guide, an art historian specializing in Byzantine art, will walk you through the 14th-century mosaics depicting the life of Christ and the Virgin Mary. We can also arrange private tours of the Basilica Cistern, the underground Byzantine water reservoir, where the atmospheric lighting and 336 columns create an otherworldly setting.

Private museum tour prices vary based on the venue, the guide, and whether after-hours access is required. A standard private tour with an expert guide starts at 1,500 TL for a 2-hour tour. After-hours access at Hagia Sophia and Topkapi Palace starts at 15,000 TL per venue, which includes the access fee, security escort, and guide. Contact us with your interests and we will create a customized cultural itinerary.
