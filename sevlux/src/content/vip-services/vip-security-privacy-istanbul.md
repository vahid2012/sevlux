For VIP clients who require personal protection or enhanced privacy during their Istanbul visit, we partner with licensed security firms to provide professional close protection officers. Our security personnel are former military or law enforcement professionals with experience protecting heads of state, business executives, and celebrities. They are trained in discreet protection — they blend in with your group while maintaining a security perimeter, rather than drawing attention with visible firearms and tactical gear.

## Close Protection Officers

Our close protection officers are the core of our security service. Each officer is a veteran of Turkish military or police special forces units, with additional training in civilian close protection from internationally recognized security academies. They are licensed by the Turkish Private Security Services, hold valid firearms permits, and are trained in first aid and emergency medical response. Most importantly, they understand that VIP security is about preventing incidents before they occur, not reacting to them — their work is proactive, discreet, and professional.

A single close protection officer is suitable for most VIP clients visiting Istanbul for tourism or business. The officer accompanies you throughout the day — at your hotel, in meetings, at restaurants, and during transportation — maintaining a discreet presence while ensuring your safety. For higher-risk situations or higher-profile clients, we can arrange a full security detail including a personal protection officer, a driver, and a security advance agent who arrives at venues before the client to assess the situation and coordinate with venue security.

All our security personnel sign non-disclosure agreements and are bound by strict confidentiality protocols. They understand that discretion is as important as protection — they will not discuss your itinerary, your identity, or any details of your visit with anyone outside the security team. Security service pricing starts at 3,500 TL per day for a single close protection officer, with full security details priced on request based on the level of protection required.

## Secure Transportation

For clients who require secure transportation, we offer a fleet of armored vehicles with security-trained drivers. Our armored vehicles include Mercedes S-Class Guard and BMW 7 Series High Security models, both certified to VR6 ballistic protection level (resistant to assault rifle fire and hand grenades). The vehicles are discreetly armored — they look identical to standard luxury vehicles from the outside — and are equipped with run-flat tires, reinforced suspension, and emergency communication systems.

Our security drivers are trained in defensive driving, evasive maneuvers, and route planning. They monitor traffic and security conditions in real time and can adjust routes to avoid potential risks. For motorcades of 2 or more vehicles, we provide a lead vehicle with a route-clearance driver and a follow vehicle with a security officer. Secure transportation pricing starts at 4,500 TL per day for a single armored vehicle with a security driver, or 8,000 TL per day for a two-vehicle motorcade.

## Privacy Arrangements

Privacy arrangements are equally important for many VIP clients. We can arrange private check-in at hotels (bypassing the lobby entirely), private dining rooms with dedicated entrances, and itineraries that avoid crowded tourist areas. For celebrities or public figures, we can coordinate with venues to ensure that photography and social media posting by staff and other guests is prohibited.

Private hotel check-in is available at most luxury hotels in Istanbul, including the Ciragan Palace Kempinski, Four Seasons Bosphorus, and the Swissotel. Our staff coordinates with the hotel to prepare your room in advance, and you are escorted directly to your room via a private entrance or elevator, where the check-in formalities are completed in your suite. This avoids any time in the lobby where you might be recognized or photographed.

For dining, we can arrange private dining rooms with dedicated entrances at Istanbul's top restaurants, so you never pass through the main dining room. For sightseeing, we can arrange early-morning or after-hours access to major attractions, when there are no other visitors present. For transportation, we can arrange for a private driver and vehicle for the duration of your stay, so that you are never dependent on taxis or public transport where you might be recognized.

Our security team will conduct a risk assessment, recommend the appropriate level of protection, and prepare a detailed security plan covering your entire visit.
