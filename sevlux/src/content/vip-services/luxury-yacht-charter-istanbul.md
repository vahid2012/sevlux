## Bosphorus Cruise Routes

A private Bosphorus yacht charter typically lasts 2 to 6 hours, with routes customized to your preferences. The standard short cruise departs from Besiktas or Eminonu and cruises north along the European shore, passing Dolmabahce Palace, the Ciragan Palace, Ortakoy Mosque, and the Bosphorus Bridge before turning back. This 2-hour route covers the most scenic and historic section of the Bosphorus and is ideal for a first-time visitor.

For a longer experience, the full Bosphorus cruise continues north past the second suspension bridge (Fatih Sultan Mehmet Bridge), the Rumeli Fortress, and the waterfront mansions of Arnavutkoy and Bebek, reaching the Black Sea entrance at Anadolu Kavagi. This 4-6 hour cruise includes a stop for lunch at a waterfront seafood restaurant in Sariyer or Poyrazkoy, accessible only by boat. For multi-day charters, we can cruise to the Princes' Islands, the Sea of Marmara, or even the Aegean coast.

## Bosphorus Sunset Cruise

Our most popular yacht charter is the Bosphorus Sunset Cruise, a 3-hour experience timed to catch the golden hour and sunset over the city. The yacht departs at 5:30 PM (6:30 PM in summer) and cruises along the European shore as the sun sets behind the Old City minarets. Champagne and canapes are served on deck as the city lights begin to illuminate both shores. The yacht passes under both Bosphorus bridges, which are lit with changing colored lights at night, before returning to port.

This is one of the most romantic and memorable experiences available in Istanbul, and it is a favorite for marriage proposals, anniversary celebrations, and special birthdays. For proposals, we can arrange roses on deck, a photographer hidden on board, and a champagne toast. The sunset cruise can be upgraded to include a private dinner on board, prepared by the yacht chef, with a customized menu and wine pairing. Sunset cruise pricing starts at 15,000 TL for a 15-meter yacht (up to 6 guests) and 45,000 TL for a 30-meter yacht (up to 20 guests), including champagne and canapes.

## Full-Day Charter with Swimming and Lunch

![Yacht sailing on the Bosphorus with Istanbul landscape](https://images.pexels.com/photos/37546642/pexels-photo-37546642.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&h=650&w=940)

For the ultimate Bosphorus experience, our full-day yacht charter departs at 10:00 AM and returns at sunset. The cruise heads north to the Black Sea entrance, anchoring at a secluded bay near Poyrazkoy for swimming and water sports. The yacht is equipped with jet skis, paddleboards, and snorkeling gear. A gourmet lunch is served on board — fresh seafood, meze, grilled meats, and seasonal fruit — or we can dock at a waterfront restaurant in Sariyer for a traditional fish lunch.

After lunch, the cruise heads south along the Asian shore, passing the Anatolian Fortress, Kucuksu Palace, and Beylerbeyi Palace. The afternoon includes another swim stop near one of the Princes' Islands or a quiet cove along the Asian shore. The day concludes with a sunset cruise back to port as the city lights illuminate the Bosphorus. Full-day charter pricing starts at 35,000 TL for a 20-meter yacht (up to 10 guests) and 90,000 TL for a 35-meter superyacht (up to 16 guests), including crew, fuel, catering, and water toys.
