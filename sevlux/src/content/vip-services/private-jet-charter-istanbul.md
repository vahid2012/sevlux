## Private Jet Fleet Options

Our private jet fleet includes several categories of aircraft, each suited to different route lengths, passenger counts, and budgets. Light jets such as the Embraer Phenom 300 and Cessna Citation CJ3 seat 6 to 8 passengers and are ideal for regional flights within Turkey or to nearby destinations like Athens, Tel Aviv, or Cairo. Midsize jets like the Hawker 800XP and Learjet 60 seat 8 to 9 passengers and offer longer range, suitable for flights from Dubai, Riyadh, or European capitals. Heavy jets and ultra-long-range aircraft like the Gulfstream G650 and Bombardier Global 6000 seat 12 to 16 passengers and can fly nonstop from Istanbul to New York, Singapore, or Cape Town.

All aircraft in our partner fleet are maintained to the highest safety standards and are certified by both the Turkish Directorate General of Civil Aviation and international aviation authorities (EASA, FAA). Each aircraft is crewed with two experienced pilots and a flight attendant who provides in-flight catering and passenger service. The cabins are configured with leather seats, work tables, WiFi (on most aircraft), and entertainment systems. For long-haul flights, many aircraft have lie-flat beds or private sleeping compartments.

A helicopter transfer from Istanbul Airport to a Bosphorus hotel takes just 12 minutes, compared to 50 minutes by car during normal traffic — and up to 90 minutes during rush hour. This service is invaluable for clients with tight schedules, connecting flights, or who simply prefer to arrive in style.
