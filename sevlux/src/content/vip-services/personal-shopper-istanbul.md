Istanbul is one of the world's great shopping destinations, with everything from historic bazaars to designer boutiques. For VIP clients who want a curated shopping experience, we offer personal shopping and styling services. Our personal shoppers are fashion industry professionals who know Istanbul's shopping landscape intimately, from the luxury boutiques of Nisantasi to the vintage shops of Cukurcuma and the artisan workshops of the Grand Bazaar.

## Luxury Shopping in Nisantasi

![Personal stylist helping a client choose clothing in a boutique](https://images.pexels.com/photos/8387126/pexels-photo-8387126.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&h=650&w=940)

A typical personal shopping day begins with a consultation at your hotel, where your stylist discusses your preferences, budget, and wardrobe needs. Based on this conversation, your stylist creates a customized shopping itinerary. In Nisantasi, Istanbul's premier luxury district, you will visit international brands like Louis Vuitton, Hermes, and Prada alongside Turkish designers like Vakko and Beymen. Your stylist can arrange private appointments with store managers, access to pieces not yet on the sales floor, and VIP fitting rooms with refreshments.

Nisantasi is also home to several Turkish jewelry designers and ateliers that are not found in any other city. Your stylist can arrange visits to these ateliers, where you can view and purchase one-of-a-kind pieces. For clients interested in watches, we have relationships with authorized dealers for Rolex, Patek Philippe, and Audemars Piguet in Istanbul, and we can arrange private viewings of limited-edition pieces. Shopping in Nisantasi is a relaxed, elegant experience — the streets are tree-lined, the cafes are excellent, and the service is personalized.

## Grand Bazaar Guided Shopping

For a more traditional shopping experience, we arrange guided shopping at the Grand Bazaar, where our personal shopper navigates the 4,000 shops and negotiates the best prices on jewelry, carpets, leather goods, and ceramics. The Grand Bazaar can be overwhelming for first-time visitors — it covers 60 streets and has more than 4,000 shops — but with our guide, you will visit only the best and most reputable shops, saving hours of wandering and ensuring that you get authentic, high-quality goods at fair prices.

We have trusted relationships with the best jewelers and carpet dealers in the bazaar, ensuring that you get authentic, high-quality goods at fair prices. For clients interested in Turkish carpets, we can arrange a private viewing at a carpet dealer's showroom, where hundreds of handmade kilims and carpets are laid out for your inspection with tea and snacks. Our carpet expert can explain the differences between regions, weaving techniques, and materials, and can arrange shipping for purchases to your home country. For jewelry, we work with goldsmiths who create custom pieces — if you have a design in mind, we can arrange a consultation and a quote.

## Vintage and Antique Shopping in Cukurcuma

![Women browsing clothing in a modern boutique](https://images.pexels.com/photos/8396309/pexels-photo-8396309.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&h=650&w=940)

The Cukurcuma neighborhood in Beyoglu is Istanbul's antique and vintage district, filled with shops selling Ottoman-era furniture, vintage clothing, antique jewelry, and collectibles. This is a favorite area for interior designers, collectors, and fashion enthusiasts looking for unique pieces that cannot be found anywhere else. Our personal shopper can guide you through the best shops, negotiate prices, and arrange shipping for large items.

Cukurcuma is also home to several up-and-coming Turkish fashion designers who have their ateliers and small boutiques in the neighborhood. These designers create limited-edition pieces that are not available in stores, and visiting their ateliers gives you the opportunity to meet the designer and see the creative process. For clients interested in Turkish fashion, this is a unique and rewarding experience.
