However, the best restaurants are often fully booked weeks in advance, especially on weekends and during the peak tourist season. Our VIP restaurant reservation service secures tables at Istanbul's most sought-after restaurants, including those that are nominally "fully booked." ## Top Istanbul Restaurants We Book

![Elegant outdoor dining table with wine glasses](https://images.pexels.com/photos/33144658/pexels-photo-33144658.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&h=650&w=940)

Our reservation service covers the full spectrum of Istanbul dining, from traditional Turkish fine dining to modern fusion cuisine. For traditional Ottoman palace cuisine, we recommend Tuti at the Ciragan Palace Kempinski, where the chef recreates 19th-century recipes from the Sultan's kitchens. The restaurant has both indoor seating and a waterfront terrace with Bosphorus views, and reservations for terrace seating at sunset must be made weeks in advance.

For modern Turkish cuisine, Mikla on the roof of the Marmara Pera hotel offers innovative dishes that combine Anatolian ingredients with contemporary techniques. The restaurant has been featured in the World's 50 Best Restaurants list and has a rooftop terrace with panoramic views of the Golden Horn and the Old City. The tasting menu with wine pairing is the recommended experience, and reservations should be made at least 2 weeks in advance.

For seafood, we recommend Balikci Sabahattin in Sultanahmet, a historic fish restaurant that has served presidents and celebrities for decades. The restaurant does not accept online reservations, but we can secure a table through our direct relationship with the owner. For Bosphorus waterfront dining, we recommend Sungrass Grill in Kurucesme, which offers premium steaks and seafood with live music on weekends. For Turkish kebabs, Zubeyir Ocakbasi near Taksim is our top recommendation — the ocakbasi (open grill) experience is theatrical and the food is exceptional.

## Private Dining and Chef's Table Experiences

For a truly exclusive experience, we can arrange private dining rooms, chef's table seating, or even after-hours dining where the restaurant is closed to other guests. A private dining room at a top Istanbul restaurant provides an intimate setting for business dinners, family celebrations, or romantic occasions. The private rooms at restaurants like Tuti, Mikla, and 16 Roof (at the Swissotel) can accommodate 8 to 20 guests and include dedicated waitstaff and customized menus.

The chef's table experience places you in the restaurant kitchen, where you can watch the chef and the team prepare your meal. This is available at several Istanbul restaurants, including Mikla and Neolokal, and includes an extended tasting menu with explanations from the chef at each course. For the ultimate exclusivity, we can arrange an after-hours dinner where the restaurant is closed to other guests and the entire venue is yours. This is particularly popular for marriage proposals, anniversary celebrations, and high-level business dinners. After-hours dining starts at 25,000 TL in addition to the meal cost.
