Arriving in a new city can be stressful — long immigration lines, confusing airports, and unreliable taxis. Our VIP airport transfer service eliminates all of this, providing a seamless, luxury arrival and departure experience at both Istanbul Airport (IST) and Sabiha Gokcen Airport (SAW). From fast-track immigration to luxury vehicles and English-speaking chauffeurs, we handle every detail so that your Istanbul journey begins and ends in comfort and style.

## Fast-Track Airport Service

For VIP clients arriving on commercial flights, our fast-track airport service transforms the arrival experience. Our meet-and-greet team meets you at the aircraft door or jet bridge as you disembark, holding a personalized sign with your name. They escort you through a dedicated fast-track lane at passport control, bypassing the regular queue which can take 30-60 minutes during peak arrival times. Your luggage is collected by our staff from the carousel and brought to you, and a luxury vehicle is waiting curbside for your transfer to the hotel. The entire process, which normally takes 45-90 minutes, is completed in under 10 minutes.

This service is available at both Istanbul Airport (IST) and Sabiha Gokcen Airport (SAW) for both arrivals and departures. For departures, we provide expedited check-in (your luggage is taken from you at the curb), fast-track security, and access to the VIP lounge where you can relax, eat, or work before your flight. A lounge pass includes comfortable seating, complimentary food and beverages, WiFi, showers, and quiet work areas. The fast-track service can be combined with our luxury car transfer for a complete door-to-door VIP experience.

Fast-track pricing starts at $350 per person for arrivals (meet-and-greet, fast-track immigration, luggage assistance) and $400 per person for departures (fast-track check-in, security, and lounge access). Children under 12 receive a 50% discount. The service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and can be booked with as little as 24 hours' notice.

Our luxury vehicle fleet is designed for the comfort and safety of VIP clients. The fleet includes Mercedes S-Class, BMW 7 Series, and Range Rover vehicles, all less than 3 years old, immaculately maintained, and equipped with WiFi, bottled water, phone chargers, and umbrellas. For larger groups, we offer Mercedes V-Class vans that seat up to 7 passengers with luggage. All vehicles are non-smoking and can be configured with child seats on request.

Our chauffeurs are professional, English-speaking drivers with years of experience serving VIP clients. They are trained in defensive driving, are knowledgeable about Istanbul's roads and traffic patterns, and understand the importance of discretion and punctuality. They monitor flight arrivals in real time and adjust their arrival at the airport accordingly, so they are always waiting for you when you land — even if your flight is early or delayed. For departures, they calculate the optimal departure time from your hotel based on traffic conditions, ensuring you arrive at the airport with ample time but without unnecessary waiting.

Luxury transfer pricing starts at 800 TL for a sedan transfer from Istanbul Airport to the city center (Sultanahmet, Beyoglu, or Besiktas) and 1,200 TL from Sabiha Gokcen. For the Mercedes V-Class van, pricing starts at 1,200 TL from Istanbul Airport and 1,800 TL from Sabiha Gokcen. Round-trip transfers receive a 15% discount. All prices include meet-and-greet, luggage assistance, WiFi, and bottled water.

## Helicopter Transfers

For the fastest possible transfer from the airport to your hotel or yacht, we arrange helicopter transfers from both Istanbul Airport and Sabiha Gokcen Airport. A helicopter transfer from Istanbul Airport to a Bosphorus hotel takes just 12 minutes, compared to 50 minutes by car during normal traffic — and up to 90 minutes during rush hour. This service is invaluable for clients with tight schedules, connecting flights, or who simply prefer to arrive in style.

Our helicopter fleet includes the Airbus EC135 and Bell 429, both configured for VIP transport with leather seating, noise-canceling headsets, and panoramic windows. The helicopters operate from dedicated helipads at both airports and can land at several hotels and private venues along the Bosphorus that have helipads. Helicopter transfer pricing starts at $1,500 for a single airport-to-city transfer (up to 5 passengers) and $2,800 from Sabiha Gokcen. Scenic helicopter flights over the Bosphorus and the city skyline start at $3,500 for a 30-minute flight.
