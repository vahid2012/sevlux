This guide covers the complete process of buying and selling hotels in Istanbul, from identifying opportunities and conducting financial due diligence to understanding tourism operation licenses, staff requirements, and regulatory compliance. We explain how hotels are valued — including RevPAR analysis, cap rates, and the premium that branded properties command. You will learn about the Tourism Investment Certificate, which unlocks tax incentives and reduced utility rates, and the transfer process for existing licenses. Whether you are acquiring a boutique hotel in Sultanahmet or a resort on the Bosphorus, this guide equips you for a sound investment.
