The Turkish hamam is a 600-year-old tradition that blends cleansing, relaxation, and social ritual into a uniquely Ottoman experience. For many visitors, it is a highlight of their Istanbul trip — but it can also be intimidating if you don't know what to expect. This guide walks you through every stage of a hamam visit, from the camekan (entrance hall) to the sicaklik (hot room) and the sogukluk (cool room). We explain the tellak (masseur) service, the kese (full-body scrub), the kopuk (foam wash), and the optional massage. We cover etiquette, what to wear, tipping customs, and how to choose between historic hamams like Cagaloglu and Cemberlitas versus modern hotel spas. With this guide, you will step into your hamam experience with confidence.
