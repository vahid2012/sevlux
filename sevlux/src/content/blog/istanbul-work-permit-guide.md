Obtaining a work permit in Istanbul is a critical step for foreigners who want to work legally in Turkey, whether as an employee or as a founder of their own company. This guide explains both the employer-sponsored route and the business-owner route, detailing the documents, timelines, and conditions for each. We cover the minimum wage requirements, the quota system that limits foreign employees to 10% of a company's workforce, and the exemptions available for certain nationalities and key personnel. You will learn about the new 2026 regulation changes, including the streamlined online application portal and reduced processing times. With clear checklists and practical tips, this guide helps you navigate the work permit process efficiently and avoid common rejection reasons.
