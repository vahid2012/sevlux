The Princes Islands (Adalar) are a cluster of nine car-free islands in the Sea of Marmara, just a short ferry ride from Istanbul's city center. A day trip to Buyukada, the largest and most popular island, feels like stepping into a different era — Victorian-era mansions, horse-drawn carriages, pine forests, and quiet beaches replace the noise and traffic of the city. This guide covers everything you need for a perfect day trip: ferry schedules from Kabatas and Kadikoy, ticket prices, what to do on the island, where to rent a bicycle, the best swimming spots, and restaurant recommendations. We also cover the smaller, quieter islands like Heybeliada and Burgazada for those who want to escape even the day-tripper crowds.
