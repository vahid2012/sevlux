Finding the right commercial space in Istanbul can make or break your business. This guide covers the commercial real estate landscape across Istanbul's key business districts, from Levent and Maslak's modern office towers to the industrial zones of Tuzla and Hadimkoy. We explain leasing versus buying, typical rent structures, and the hidden costs that catch foreign tenants by surprise — such as the KDV (VAT) on commercial rent and common area maintenance fees. For retail businesses, we cover the best shopping streets and mall locations by foot traffic and target demographic. For manufacturers and importers, we discuss warehouse and factory options near ports and logistics hubs. Practical negotiation tips help you secure favorable terms.
