Choosing where to stay in Istanbul dramatically shapes your experience of the city. Each neighborhood has its own character, advantages, and trade-offs. Sultanahmet puts you walking distance from the Blue Mosque and Hagia Sophia but can feel touristy at night. Beyoglu and Galata offer vibrant dining and nightlife but are hilly and busier. Besiktas and Ortakoy provide Bosphorus charm and central convenience. Kadikoy on the Asian side gives you an authentic, less touristy Istanbul with great food scenes. This guide compares each neighborhood by location, atmosphere, hotel options, price range, transport connections, and suitability for different traveler types. We help you match your priorities — sightseeing, nightlife, budget, or authenticity — to the right area.
